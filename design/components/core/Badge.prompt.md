Small pill label for discounts, statuses, and inline ratings.

```jsx
<Badge tone="sale">-20%</Badge>
<RatingBadge value="4.7" />
```

`Badge` tones: `sale` (red, discount tags), `info` (blue), `success` (green, in-stock), `neutral` (gray, default). `RatingBadge` renders a filled star + numeric rating, used next to seller/product names.
