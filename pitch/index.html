02 / Our Solution

Hustlr: All-in-One Commerce Operating System

Instant storefronts, verified milestone settlements, and an AI-powered co-founder.

1. Multi-Tenant Architecture

Instant subdomains, custom domains, and isolated data schemas. Zero cross-store data leakage, ensuring absolute merchant privacy and frictionless guest checkouts.

2. Escrow Trust Engine

Funds are authenticated via Paystack webhook and held securely. Payout is instantly cleared only when the buyer confirms delivery, eliminating fraud for both parties.

3. AI Seller Co-Founder

A private, tenant-isolated AI partner. It auto-generates SEO catalog metadata and acts as an autonomous CFO for financial queries and inventory restock predictions.
