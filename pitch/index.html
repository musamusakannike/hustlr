Core Trust Engine

Built-In Automated Escrow & Settlements

Eliminating payment friction and chargeback risks for both buyers and merchants.

1. Payment Lockdown

Funds are authenticated via Paystack webhook (HMAC-SHA512) and locked into platform escrow before dispatch.

2. Delivery Verification & Release

Buyer confirms satisfaction upon delivery to instantly release funds directly into the merchant's available wallet balance.

3. Automated 48-Hour Fallback Cron

Unresponsive buyers cannot hold funds hostage; automated cron jobs release payments safely after 48 hours unless a dispute is active.
